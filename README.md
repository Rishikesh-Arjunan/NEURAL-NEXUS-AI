# 🧠 Neural Nexus AI GAME

### 🌐 AI World • Future Simulation • Strategic Decisions

> **Enter the AI world. Make decisions. Complete missions. Shape the future.**

Neural Nexus AI is an interactive **AI-powered future simulation experience** where users enter a virtual neural world, make strategic decisions, complete missions, and progress through different levels.

---

## 🚀 Features

- 🧠 **Interactive AI World**
- ⚡ **Energy System**
- ⭐ **XP & Level Progression**
- 🎯 **AI Score Tracking**
- ⚔️ **Multiple Strategic Decisions**
  - Attack
  - Defend
  - Scan
  - Hack
- 🎮 **Mission-Based Gameplay**
- 🤖 **AI Prediction System**
- 🌌 **Modern Futuristic UI**
- 📱 **Responsive Web Design**

---

## 🎮 How It Works

1. 🚀 Start a Mission
2. 🌐 Enter the Neural World
3. 🎯 Choose your AI Decision
4. 🤖 Use AI Prediction
5. ⭐ Earn XP and improve your level
6. 🏆 Build your AI Score
7. 🔥 Continue exploring the future

---
